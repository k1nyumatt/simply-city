

                                                                    SIMPLYCITY

Building simplycity, a solution towards ordeliness in the transport sector and improving urban mobility by informing online drivers/riders all the legal pickup and drop off zones in the CBD.

simplycity aims at simplifying the knowledge of the cbd and reducing the conflicts between county askaris and online drivers who seek to pick and drop their clients in the cbd.
a driver's companion to help him/her navigate the city with ease.

the backend includes accounts where the users will need to create accounts and set up passwords that are only unique to them and mashimo which holds a map that has locations of all the drop off/pick up zones in the cbd.
